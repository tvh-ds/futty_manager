# Scout

Scout describes football performance evidence and supports recruitment and lineup planning.

## Data and ratings

**Master snapshot**: A frozen union of source observations for a performance season, including identities, selected values and their provenance.

**Snapshot revision**: A complete successor to a master snapshot after an explicitly identified transformation; the predecessor remains available.

**Observed zero**: A zero supplied by source evidence, distinct from an unavailable observation labelled zero for presentation.

**Zero-filled feature**: An unrecorded measurement represented as zero with explicit missing-evidence status; it is not an observed zero.

**Compatible peers**: Players whose role, observation window, measurement definitions and effective feature weights support the same rating comparison.

**Ability calibration**: Comparison of weighted performance evidence against compatible peers to produce an ability rating and explain its contributions.

## Squad planning

**Planning lineup**: A user's chosen formation, unique player assignments and bench, rather than an official starting eleven.

**Planning revision**: One accepted state of the planning lineup and notes; later edits supersede pending work based on earlier states.

**Authoritative evaluation**: Ratings and familiarity evidence calculated by the engine for one identified planning lineup.
