# Scout

Scout is a personal football recruitment engineering portfolio. Its users are analysts and coaches investigating a position, replacement, or squad need. Success means selecting a team, editing a recruitment brief, inspecting evidence and unknowns, comparing candidates, running reproducible scenarios, and exporting an auditable decision record.

The product covers men's senior football and eight role families including goalkeepers. Real candidate releases require all five major European leagues and a common completed season of 2024/25 or later. Data must be free and public display rights must be documented. No paid source or older candidate cohort is an automatic substitute. Historical research is separate.

Quality, style, tactical fit, replacement fit and evidence coverage are separate. Missing measurements stay missing. Unknown hard constraints require verification. Projections are assumption-driven scenarios, not causal transfer forecasts. User notes and watchlists stay in the browser. The optional language model interprets an editable brief and has no authority to execute queries or score players.

The approved stack is React/TypeScript, FastAPI/Python, PostgreSQL, PySpark/Delta Lake, Databricks, MLflow, evaluated classical ML and a bounded PyTorch experiment. Azure hosts the application using existing credits; deployment must preserve spending protections. No credentials are configured in this workspace at initialization. Synthetic demonstration data must be unmistakably labelled and must never certify real-data coverage.

The main interface is an analytical workbench: brief, ranked evidence, player comparison and squad scenarios. Prioritize legibility, keyboard operation, narrow-screen usability and actionable failure states. Implementation is authorized by the user; use the approved workflow and make routine visual decisions without another approval round.
