# Eight role walkthroughs

These are **software demonstration recipes** using fictional candidates, not football scouting verdicts or external-reviewed case studies. Each follows team → role/replacement → candidates → evidence → comparison → scenario → export. An authentic portfolio case study still needs real approved data, personal relevance judgments, sensitivity analysis and recorded evidence.

| Role | Brief to try | Evidence/unknowns to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| GK | Keeper who supports build-up | Save share, distribution fields, missing shot-difficulty adjustment; do not infer shot prevention from save share alone. |
| CB | Left-footed defender comfortable with a high line | Foot constraint, progression/duels, and explicit athletic/high-line/pressure verification questions. |
| FB/WB | Wide defender contributing progression | Carries/passing/defensive rates; instructions and physical recovery remain unmeasured. |
| DM | Midfielder screening space and progressing possession | Defensive events and progression; event counts do not directly measure space protection. |
| CM | Central midfielder linking play | Passing/carrying/activity profile; edit intended style and compare observed coverage. |
| AM | Creator replacing a departing attacking midfielder | Chance-creation metrics and preserved/changed functions; raw direction labels are not causal improvements. |
| W | Winger providing progression and threat | Take-ons, carries/creation/shots; side, off-ball runs and pressing quality need additional evidence. |
| ST | Forward offering shot volume and contribution | Goals/shots/role style, exposure shrinkage and ranges; finishing quality needs richer historical shot context. |

For each role, retain the data/feature/scoring versions, full brief, uncertainty, unsupported attributes and scenario seed in the export. Use the 48-brief suite for development versus held-out judgments; do not fill relevance ratings automatically from the same ranking score being evaluated.
